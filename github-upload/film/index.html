      About

      I'm Nadia, a creative based in Bali. I work on both sides of the camera: I come up with the idea, I'm in the frame, and I shape it in the edit.

      Every project starts with a mood. A place, a piece of clothing, a song. From there I build the movement, the look and the rhythm, so the final piece feels like a world of its own.
